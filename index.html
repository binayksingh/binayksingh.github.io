I am a Graduate Teaching Assistant and PhD Candidate at the Department of Computer Science at the University of Central Florida (UCF), working under the supervision of Dr. Niels Da Vitoria Lobo.

My core research lives at the intersection of deep learning and computer vision architectures, with a hyper-focus on designing next-generation systems for robust real-time object detection, contextual feature-fusion pipelines, and semi-supervised representation learning models.
